# Myanmar's Ghost Capital: Who Needs a 20-Lane Road?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Myanmar moved its administrative capital in 2005. Reporters visiting in 2015 described widely separated districts, hotels and government offices and boulevards up to 20 lanes across. The account also describes occupied places and people doing their jobs [1,2].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Population inside any displayed frame, project cost and claims of universal desertion.

## Scope and treatment

Follow a fictional walker rather than asserting the capital is empty. Distinguish photographed traffic flow from administrative population. Walk from a quiet road to a workplace; explain zoning, distance, and why road capacity and demand need different evidence. Avoid speculation about leaders' strategic intent, aviation anecdotes or current conflict.
