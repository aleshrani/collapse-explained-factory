# US Psychic Spies: What Happened When Someone Checked the Answers?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The operational summary records more than 200 tasks from 1986 through the first quarter of fiscal year 1995 [1]. CIA describes early research and a handoff to military intelligence, then an independent review delivered in September 1995 [2]. Reviewers found output too vague for actionable intelligence [3].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Exact total programme cost and claims that any named case demonstrated clairvoyance.

## Scope and treatment

Illustrate with an invented drawing of a circle matched after the fact to a clock or a plate. Explain blind judging, the difference between advance predictions and retrospective matches, why a vague report is expensive to check, and what an operational customer needs. Label all imaginary demonstrations. No claims of paranormal ability.
