# Nicaragua Canal: The Concession Ended Before the Canal Opened

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Nicaragua's official gazette records repeal in May 2024 of the concession agreement published in June 2013 [1]. The National Assembly describes reform of the canal law in its own account [2]. Repeal of the concession and abandonment of every future route are different propositions.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Individual motives, private financing, a future canal's viability or cost.

## Scope and treatment

Start with the legal document that actually changed. Explain what a concession allows without promising financing, completed work or customers. Compare drawing a route through a lake with managing a working waterway. Discuss public questions about freshwater and local access as questions. Return to a transit ticket that cannot yet be used. End on Dubai's opposite problem: real land waiting for connections.
