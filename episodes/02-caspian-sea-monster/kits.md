Catalog IDs: caspian, ship, blueprint, map, documents, timeline, boardroom, studio, skyline.

Recurring object: a boarding pass stamped AIR and SEA.

Start on the physical caspian model. Map pins identify the project's actual location. The document desk uses the source and date attached to the current claim.
