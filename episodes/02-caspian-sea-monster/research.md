# Caspian Sea Monster: Why This Giant Flew So Low

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The KM was an experimental Soviet wing-in-ground-effect craft studied on the Caspian Sea [1]. Published dimensions give length about 92 metres and maximum takeoff mass 544 tonnes. Trials began in 1966; the prototype was lost in 1980 [2]. IMO describes three regulatory types of WIG craft [3].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Performance records, service deployments, intelligence motives inferred from pictures.
