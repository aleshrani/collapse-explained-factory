# Camp Century: The Greenland Base That Moved With the Ice

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The US Army constructed Camp Century in 1959. The base was abandoned in 1967 [1,2]. In April 2024 an airborne NASA radar survey imaged features of the base under at least 30 metres of accumulated ice and snow. Earlier radar surveys had detected signs of the base [1].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Unconditional future exposure date, exact buried inventory, claims NASA first discovered it.

## Scope and treatment

Distinguish the actual base from larger proposals. Explain accumulation versus ice movement, a radar return versus a photograph, and why a burial depth today cannot by itself predict future exposure. Discuss wastes as a monitoring question. The story centres on the physical base and the people responsible for it, without military siting or operational detail.
