# Dubai World Islands: What Happened After the Map Was Built?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

About 300 artificial islands were designed as a world map. Van Oord describes its reclamation contract [1]. Haskoning documents an environmental assessment, utility planning and plots of 2.3–8.4 hectares [2]. A project hydrographer described a site 4 km offshore and an envelope roughly 7 by 9 km in 2007 [3]. The resort factsheet dates Anantara's opening to 18 December 2021 [4]. Minor Hotels confirmed the property's closure in April 2026 [5].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Exact final sand volume, percentage sold, current occupancy and universal diesel-only claims.

## Scope and treatment

The shoreline and the development are different achievements. Show a grocery crate riding a boat. A lamp, fresh water and a replacement fridge have to arrive somehow. Explain offshore surveying, the underwater part of the shore, why shelter and flushing compete, why individual developers need shared connections, and how the fate of one operator differs from the land. One resort's closure cannot establish why each separate plot was delayed.
