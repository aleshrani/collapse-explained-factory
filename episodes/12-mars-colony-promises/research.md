# Mars Colony Promises: What Happened to the 2024 Plan?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

SpaceX's abridged 2017 presentation described a target of at least two cargo ships in 2022, then two cargo and two crew ships in 2024 [1]. Both dates were goals. NASA explains that favourable Mars launch opportunities recur roughly every 26 months [2]. Neither of the targeted human missions happened in 2024.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Any future landing date, exact cost per settler and private finances.

## Scope and treatment

Treat dates as dated ambitions. Distinguish an announcement, an Earth test, a Mars landing, and a settlement that persists between deliveries. Use a departure board and a hypothetical failed refrigerator as an illustration. No speculation about motives or anecdotes attributed to Elon Musk. End with the evidence a viewer should look for next.
