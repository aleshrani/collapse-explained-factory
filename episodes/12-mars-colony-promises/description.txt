In a public plan from twenty seventeen, people were supposed to get a chance at Mars in twenty twenty-four. Mars kept the appointment.

A launch window on a calendar is a strange kind of deadline. A dated SpaceX presentation and NASA’s explanation of Mars launch opportunities put the early promises in context.
