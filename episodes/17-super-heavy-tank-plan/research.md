# Giant Tank Plan: Why a Thousand Tons Stayed on Paper

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Historical summaries associate a thousand-tonne land-cruiser study with 1942; the details of the paper proposal are disputed and there was no completed Ratte [1]. The Tank Museum documents physical evidence from the separate Maus project [2]. The museum's artifact is evidence about Maus only.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Specific drawings, dimensions, authorisations and quotations attributed to political leaders.

## Scope and treatment

Focus on evidence quality and the difference between an artist's impression, proposal and surviving object. Compare an empty display label with an actual catalogued museum artifact. Discuss the transport and maintenance implications of an immense hypothetical mass without engineering a weapon. No insignia, armament specifications or operational reconstruction.
