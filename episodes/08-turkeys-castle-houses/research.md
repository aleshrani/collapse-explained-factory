# Burj Al Babas: Who Wanted 732 Matching Castles?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Reuters described a planned 732 homes, a mall and a hotel near Mudurnu. Work halted during 2018. In its February 2019 reporting it attributed higher costs to permitting delays and the falling currency [1]. A 2024 local retrospective put partially built villas at 587 by late 2018 [2].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Today's occupancy, resale values, unfinished interiors in every house.

## Scope and treatment

Follow an imagined delivery along identical streets. Explain why repeating a design can save drawings while repeating the same costs, why a common amenity depends on enough buyers, how imported material prices can move before a house finishes. Keep buyers' real outcomes and current proceedings out. Distinguish houses started from keys delivered.
