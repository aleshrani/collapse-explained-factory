# Zumwalt Destroyer: How a Fleet of 32 Became Three Ships

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

GAO describes the change from a planned 32 ships to 16, then eight, and ultimately three as the programme evolved [1]. A later GAO report distinguishes modernisation spending from original procurement and documents changes to the ships' planned equipment [2]. Twenty-nine is arithmetic on planned quantities, not a count of scrapped ships.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

An all-in per-ship price without a stated denominator, uncertain future capabilities.

## Scope and treatment

Follow an order slip rather than a combat sequence. Explain fixed vs recurring costs with a hypothetical mould, why small fleets have unusual supply chains, and how a modernisation decision differs from an original design choice. Use a generic dockyard silhouette; do not illustrate weapons operation or tactics.
