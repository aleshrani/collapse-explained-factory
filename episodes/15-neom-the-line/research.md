# The Line: What Happens When a City Is 170 Kilometres Long?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund published the design in July 2022: 170 km long, 200 m wide, a 34-square-kilometre footprint and an eventual target of nine million people [1]. These are proposal figures. A 2023 delivery-partner announcement repeated the dimensions and population target [2].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Rumoured cost overruns, construction completion dates and current headcounts.

## Scope and treatment

Compare a short daily trip to a long crosstown visit. The rectangle area is a calculation, not a built footprint. Explain access, interchange and scale using a ruler and a baker's delivery trip. Label all generic diagrams as planned or illustrative. A funding announcement cannot verify occupancy. No forecast presented as an observed change.
