# Akon City: What Became of the City Announced in Senegal?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Senegal's public broadcaster dates the launch to 2020 in Mbodiène and describes planned hospitality and other facilities [1]. In June 2025 SAPCO's director told APS the original project was no longer current. He described a new eight-hectare proposal being finalized, distinct from the city [2]. Five years is the difference between the two years.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Money raised, losses, motives and accusations against living people.

## Scope and treatment

Treat the public launch as a proposal and SAPCO's interview as an attributed update. Measure eight hectares using a hypothetical rectangle and distinguish land area from completed floor area. Separate local infrastructure needs from celebrity imagery. Ask what an observer can verify about a smaller proposal without implying it opened.
