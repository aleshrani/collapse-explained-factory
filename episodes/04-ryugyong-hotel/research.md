# Ryugyong Hotel: How an Empty Hotel Became a Giant Screen

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

AP reported from Pyongyang in December 2018 that the 105-storey unfinished hotel was showing animations on its facade. Work began in 1987 and the tower was tied to preparations for a youth festival. The main show ran about four minutes [1,2].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Current interior condition, future opening dates and independent room counts.

## Scope and treatment

Tell the case from a room key's perspective. Separate shell, enclosure, lighting and guest operation. Imagine a blank guest survey and a projection designer timing an image to the outer walls; these are illustrative. Explain why observers can verify a facade more easily than interior services.
