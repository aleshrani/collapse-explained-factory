In twenty twenty-four San Francisco was still publishing a map of a building's movement where the date beside the ruler mattered as much as the number on it.

A sequence of survey readings can tell a resident more than a single dramatic number. San Francisco’s monitoring reports show what was measured, when and against which reference.
