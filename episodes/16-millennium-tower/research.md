# Millennium Tower: What a Tilt Measurement Actually Means

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The city's instrumentation reports document the perimeter pile upgrade and repeated monitoring at named stations. A September 2023 report lists staged load-transfer dates [1]. The June 2024 contour map reports changes from May 2021 through June 2024 [2]. Changes in that interval are not lifetime totals.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Present tilt without a dated dataset, personal fault and ongoing litigation.

## Scope and treatment

Follow two marks on opposite sides of a tabletop. Equal settlement moves both down; differential settlement changes the slope. Explain stable reference points, roof offset versus base settlement and measurement intervals. The resident deserves a date with a measurement. No diagnoses of present safety or legal allegations.
