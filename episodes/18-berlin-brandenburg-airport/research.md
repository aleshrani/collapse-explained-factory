# Berlin Brandenburg Airport: Why Opening Day Kept Moving

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The airport operator records Terminal 1 opening on 31 October 2020 with two arriving aircraft and Tegel closing on 8 November [1]. Its 2012 annual report says a postponement decision was made on 8 May 2012 and identifies technical fire-protection problems [2]. Its chronology also records cancellation of a planned October 2013 opening [1].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Unverified counts of faults and workers, blame assigned to individuals.

## Scope and treatment

Follow a boarding pass between a building that looks finished and an airport cleared to open. Explain commissioning and integrated testing at a broad level. Different systems have to agree that the whole terminal is ready. Revisit the real arrivals for a concrete ending. Avoid allegations about individual qualifications or ongoing disputes.
