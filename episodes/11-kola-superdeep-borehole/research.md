# Kola Superdeep Borehole: What Came Back From Twelve Kilometres Down?

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The scientific literature reports a maximum depth of 12,262 metres for SG-3 [1]. The same paper describes temperature and heat-flow measurements and use of an 8,578-metre borehole as a geolaboratory. GFZ describes the Kola borehole as the deepest and gives a slightly different final depth [2]. Use about 12.3 km when comparing accounts.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

A single universally agreed bottom temperature, exact uniform bore diameter and folklore about recordings.

## Scope and treatment

Describe the difference between depth and drilled length. Treat the hole as an instrument rather than a tunnel people enter. A core is a local sample; a seismic profile covers far more space but needs interpretation. Explain why heat in rock differs from the temperature a just-drilled hole records. Close with the labelled tray and a more honest geological map.
