# CIA Mining Ship: The Real Job Was a Sunken Submarine

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

CIA's museum documents a six-year effort following the 1968 loss of a Soviet submarine. The Hughes Glomar Explorer reached the site on 4 July 1974 [1]. CIA says the wreck broke during ascent and only part was retrieved. The crew held a burial at sea for the Soviet sailors recovered [1,2].

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Unpublished intelligence, precise recovered contents and total cost.

## Scope and treatment

Treat the mining story as historical context. Focus on the gap between a publicly visible ship and the job on its paperwork, scale and uncertainty of deep-sea industrial lifting, and how the recovery ended. Give the dead and their families space. The later refusal to confirm or deny is a linguistic afterlife. No procedures for clandestine recovery, concealment or evasion.
