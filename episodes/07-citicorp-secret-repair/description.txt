In nineteen seventy-eight a Manhattan office tower was quietly reinforced, and decades later a second set of engineers challenged the best-known explanation for the repair.

A church occupies the corner where an office tower might usually put a support. City records and later wind research show how that choice shaped the history of the 1978 repair.
