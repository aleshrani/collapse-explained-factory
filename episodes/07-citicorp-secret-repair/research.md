# Citicorp Center: Why Engineers Worked After Dark

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

The city landmark report documents the unusual mid-face supports, church and 1978 strengthening [1]. NIST describes the corner-wind question, the original account and later analysis using wind measurements from a building of similar proportions [2,3]. The later authors found face winds could govern and explicitly qualify their conclusion.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

The famous one-in-sixteen figure as an established annual collapse probability.

## Scope and treatment

Use a coaster to show the same wind meeting a face or corner. Explain loads travel via the frame to supports, then the difference between an emergency decision under uncertainty and a retrospective model. Do not claim a student personally saved the city. Talk through what measurements were unavailable to later researchers, and the responsibility to act with the data you have.
