# Nuclear Tank Plan: The Reactor Was Only an Option

## Verified numerical claims and attribution

Historical reporting identifies Chrysler's TV-8 as a 1950s concept with a model pictured in R. P. Hunnicutt's history. A nuclear power source was one proposal considered, not an installed or tested system [1,2]. The design did not enter production.

## Unverified — exclude from narration and artwork

Nuclear performance, trials and claims that a functioning nuclear tank existed.

## Scope and treatment

Keep this a history of a paper project and institutional evaluation. Use a restaurant options card to explain why listing an ingredient proves neither purchase nor a meal. Discuss maintenance commitments in general terms. Do not describe reactor construction, weapon systems or operational procedures. No fantasy test footage.
